How to test it in front of the judges
  1. Flames: show a lit candle or lighter to the camera, or play a fire video on your phone and hold it up. The flame meter rises and the dashboard raises a critical alert with a photo.
  2. People: turn on the AI (needs internet once to download the model), then stand in front of the camera. A blue box appears.
  3. Injured person: with the AI on, lie on the floor in view of the camera and stay still for 5 seconds. The box turns red.
  4. Animals: hold up a phone showing a video of an elephant, a cow, a horse, a sheep, a dog or a bird. The page counts each species and tracks its movement.
  5. Lost people: tick "Roaming" on a phone or laptop with GPS. Every person sighting is sent with a photo and the GPS position.
  6. If the room light is bad, use the test buttons on the right. They send a labelled test detection, so say that it is a test.

Where is this camera?

The default point is inside the Uttarakhand sensor network, so this camera shows up on the same map. Sensors within 25 km also add "camera sees flames" to their fire risk.
