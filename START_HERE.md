# Start here (5 steps)

1. **Install Python 3.10 to 3.12** from python.org. Tick **"Add python.exe to PATH"**.
2. **Unzip** the project. Open the folder, click the address bar, type `cmd`, press Enter.
3. Type **`run.bat`** and press Enter. Wait for `Uvicorn running on http://0.0.0.0:8000` (first time takes a few minutes). Leave the window open.
4. Open **http://localhost:8000** in Chrome or Edge.
   * Click **Landslide at Joshimath** in the right panel and watch the risk rise.
   * **Phone number:** in "Get alerts on your phone" type your number, tick the box, press **Send me alerts**, then **Send test**.
5. **Laptop camera:** open **http://localhost:8000/camera.html**, press **Start camera**, click **Allow**, and show a flame (candle, or a fire video on your phone).
